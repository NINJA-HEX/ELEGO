The Pyramid of Fragrance

Understanding Scent Notes

An ELEGO perfume unfolds in three harmonious phases on the skin over time.

01

Top Notes

First 15 to 30 Minutes

The initial rush upon application. Radiant, volatile essential oils like Calabrian Bergamot, Pink Pepper, and Neroli that awaken the senses.

Key Ingredients: Bergamot, Grapefruit, Saffron, Cardamom
Core Identity
02

Heart Notes

2 to 5 Hours Wear

The soul of the fragrance. Full-bodied florals, rich aromatics, and velvet spices that bloom as the top notes gently dissipate.

Key Ingredients: Damask Rose, Jasmine Sambac, Iris, Cacao
03

Base Notes

6 to 14+ Hours Wear

The deep, lingering trail. Heavier molecules that anchor the scent onto skin, leaving a captivating, memorable sillage.

Key Ingredients: Aged Oud, Bourbon Vanilla, Sandalwood, Amber
